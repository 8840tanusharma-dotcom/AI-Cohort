SECTION 2: PREVENTIVE AND PRIMARY CARE SERVICES
================================================================================

1. Preventive Care Services (ACA Compliant):
   - Annual routine physical exam, well-child visits, and standard immunizations: Covered at 100% ( copay, deductible waived across all plans when using in-network providers).
   - Routine screenings: Mammography, colonoscopy, Pap smear, diabetes screening, lipid panels covered with no member cost-sharing for age-appropriate guidelines.

2. Primary Care Physician (PCP) Office Visits:
   - Gold PPO (P101):  copay per visit; deductible does not apply.
   - Silver HMO (P102):  copay per visit; deductible does not apply.
   - Bronze HMO (P103):  copay per visit; deductible does not apply.

3. Specialist Consultations:
   - Gold PPO (P101):  copay per visit (In-Network); 30% coinsurance after deductible (Out-of-Network).
   - Silver HMO (P102):  copay per visit with PCP referral.
   - Bronze HMO (P103):  copay per visit with PCP referral.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: DIAGNOSTIC TESTING, IMAGING, AND LABORATORY SERVICES
================================================================================

1. Laboratory & Pathology Tests:
   - Blood tests, urinalysis, cultures: Covered at plan coinsurance rate after annual deductible.
   - Independent diagnostic laboratory facilities preferred over hospital-based outpatient labs.

2. Diagnostic Imaging:
   - Routine X-Rays: Covered subject to copay or coinsurance (e.g.,  copay on Gold PPO; 20% coinsurance on Silver HMO after deductible).
   - Advanced Imaging (MRI, CT Scans, PET Scans): Subject to mandatory Pre-Authorization. Covered at plan coinsurance after annual deductible.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: HOSPITALIZATION, SURGERY, AND EMERGENCY SERVICES
================================================================================

1. Inpatient Hospital Stays:
   - Semi-private room, board, intensive care, and general nursing care: Plan coinsurance applies after deductible.
   - Prior authorization required for all non-emergency hospital admissions at least 5 business days prior to admission.

2. Outpatient Surgery:
   - Ambulatory surgery centers and hospital outpatient surgical suites: Plan coinsurance applies after annual deductible.

3. Emergency Room (ER) Care:
   - In-network and out-of-network emergency room visits: Covered at  copay per visit, waived if admitted directly to the hospital within 24 hours. Plan coinsurance applies to physician charges.

4. Urgent Care Centers:
   - Gold PPO:  copay per visit.
   - Silver HMO:  copay per visit.
   - Bronze HMO:  copay per visit.

SECTION 5: PRESCRIPTION DRUG FORMULARY
================================================================================

Prescription drugs are classified into four tiers:
- Tier 1 (Generic Drugs):  copay per 30-day supply (Retail) /  copay per 90-day supply (Mail Order).
- Tier 2 (Preferred Brand Name):  copay per 30-day supply.
- Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Brand Name):  copay per 30-day supply.
- Tier 4 (Specialty Biologics & Injectables): 25% coinsurance up to a maximum of  per prescription. Requires prior authorization and specialty pharmacy dispensing.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: EXCLUSIONS AND GENERAL LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

The following services are not covered under standard medical benefit plans unless explicitly mandated by law or rider:
- Cosmetic procedures and elective surgeries not medically necessary.
- Non-emergency services received outside the designated provider network (for HMO plans).
- Experimental, investigational, or unapproved clinical trials not accredited by recognized health authorities.
- Adult routine dental and vision correction exams (available through optional supplementary dental/vision riders).
- Custodial long-term nursing home care.
